Party games for the TV and everyone's phones. The TV shows the game and a QR code; everyone scans it, types a name and plays on their own phone. Nobody installs anything.

`tvpipe game play NAME` downloads a game and starts it; `tvpipe game play` alone shows these games in its window to pick one. **Play now** runs a game in this browser instead, with nothing to install ([Playing in the browser](in-the-browser.md)). How playing works: [Party games](party.md). Your own game on this page: [Adding a game](party.md#s5).
